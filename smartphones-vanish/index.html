What if every smartphone vanished forever?

Keys: ← → space · R restart

One morning…
every smartphone
is gone.

Hour 1

Payments freeze

Mobile wallets, QR codes and banking apps are gone. Shops simply stop.

Hour 2

Millions locked out

Your 2-factor codes went to your phone. Email, bank, work: access denied.

Hour 3

Nobody can find anyone

No maps. No contacts. How many numbers do you know by heart?

Day 1
0%

of emergency calls came from mobile phones. Landlines and radios matter again.

Week 1

The comeback of old tech

Landlines, paper maps, cash and wristwatches are back.

Month 1

Tech giants crash

Phone-first apps lose their platform. Laptop makers boom.

Year 1

The laptop is king again

Less scrolling, more planned meetups: "See you at 6, by the clock."

Hardest hit

Billions lose their only computer

For much of the world, the phone was the bank, the school and the clinic.

The catch

We'd just build them again…

…unless they keep vanishing. Then the future is watches, glasses and desks.

Would you survive without yours?
